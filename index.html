From AI experiments to enterprise intelligence

Architectural principles

  • Capability before agency

    Prove a tool does its job reliably before an agent decides when to use it.

  • Execution before reasoning

    Get the workflow running end to end, then add model judgement where it improves the result.

  • Autonomy by delegation

    Agents act on authority that people and policy have handed them, and that authority can be narrowed or withdrawn.

  • Enterprise context matters

    Master data, authorisations and process rules shape the right answer as much as the model does.

  • Evidence before trust

    Usage, accuracy and incident data decide how far a system is trusted.

  • Governance by design

    Access, audit and human oversight go into the architecture from the start.

ZAKS Drive

SAP can store documents on an HTTP content server, but SharePoint does not speak that protocol. ZAKS Drive is a small Go gateway that does, so SAP documents can live under the Microsoft 365 security, retention and backup a company already runs.
