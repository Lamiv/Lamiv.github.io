Enterprise AI integration

AI changes operations only when it acts in the systems where work is recorded.

I connect AI to SAP and enterprise APIs so it works inside existing workflows, authorisations and validation.

Production

Customer-service agents that sort enquiries, complaints and release orders into SAP workflows.

02

AI platform architecture

Enterprises stall after a few pilots when every use case rebuilds integration, context and controls.

I design shared foundations, covering orchestration, memory and reusable business capabilities, that new use cases build on.

Engineering in progress

LEO, an enterprise AI orchestration and memory architecture. Not deployed as a whole.

03

Governed AI execution

Leadership, Risk and Audit need to know what an AI system did, on whose authority, and how well.

I build agents with human oversight and monitoring that feeds governance reporting.

Production

Agents monitored after go-live for usage, accuracy and incidents, with status and risk reported to governance forums.
